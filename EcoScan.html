Waste sorting quick guide

🧴

Plastic

Empty and rinse containers. Check if your local program accepts that type of plastic.

📄

Paper

Keep paper clean and dry. Greasy or food-soiled paper may not be recyclable.

🥫

Metal

Empty cans and follow local collection guidance. Handle sharp edges carefully.

🍌

Organic

Food scraps may be composted where a suitable collection or composting system exists.

🍾

Glass

Check local glass collection rules. Wrap broken glass safely and never put it loose in a bag.

🔋

Special waste

Batteries, bulbs, chemicals, and e-waste may need dedicated collection points.
